# Value Offer Economics Signal

Integrated from the value-packaging engineering work.

## Principle

Presentation can change perceived value, but a price increase is not automatically a business win. The system must evaluate incremental contribution, not appearance alone.

## Deterministic contract

agent-skills exposes:

- `value-offer-contract.mjs`
- normalized offer variants
- unit contribution margin
- gross margin rate
- break-even conversion rate for cost recovery
- incremental contribution per visitor
- evidence gate for measured-lift claims

## Integration

- EASY: Product DNA/Product Integrity → presentation variant → offer economics → controlled test.
- MONY: package presentation upgrades as a measurable service/revenue lane.
- Elite/ARMY-14: distinguish generated visual quality from commercial outcome and recognized revenue.

## Evidence rule

No universal price multiplier is assumed. Recent research supports the general possibility that packaging/design affects willingness to pay, but results are context-dependent. Claims must be tied to measured outcomes for the specific product, audience, channel, and variant.
