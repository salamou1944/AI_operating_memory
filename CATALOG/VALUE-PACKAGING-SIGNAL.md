# Value Packaging Signal

Added as a reusable MONY/EASY capability: perceived-value engineering through truthful positioning, brand, packaging, story, photography/video, unboxing and measured offer experiments.

Evidence indicates packaging experience and design can affect willingness to pay in context, but there is no universal price multiplier. The capability therefore requires Product DNA/Product Integrity preservation and measured conversion/margin evidence.

Integration targets: EASY Creative Engine and seller offers; MONY product-presentation service lane; Elite/ARMY-14 hypothesis/evidence evaluation.
