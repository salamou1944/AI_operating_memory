# Referral Brokerage Signal

A viable MONY revenue lane is referral brokerage: connect a verified buyer with a verified service provider and earn a pre-agreed commission after the contractual earning trigger.

Important mechanics:
- lead registration and attribution
- protection window
- written commission terms
- provider acceptance
- payment-triggered revenue recognition
- commission reconciliation
- privacy/consent and local compliance checks

Use alongside affiliate revenue, not as a replacement.

Primary use cases:
- web development
- AI automation
- marketing
- design
- business software implementation

Do not use fake leads, spam, self-referrals, undisclosed conflicts or unlicensed regulated brokerage.
